02 / Deep pocket

More recessed

Changed from selected design: vertical inset increases from 3px to 4px. A recessed channel shadow and tiny tile shadow add depth; outer height and 4px gaps stay unchanged.

03 / Matte inset

Favorite

Changed from selected design: remove face gradients and the raised highlight. Flat dark faces receive a shallow inner shadow; the active task keeps its stronger fill and familiar 2px left edge. Spacing stays exactly selected.

04 / Tight seams

Denser fit

Changed from selected design: vertical inset drops to 2px, gaps to 3px, and corners to 2px. Idle borders become slightly more defined; the chosen gradient and full-height active edge remain.

Gentler active edge

Changed from selected design: corners increase to 4px and fine borders become a little stronger. Quick Launch and process access get matching beveled frames; the active marker becomes a short 2px inset stroke instead of touching the tile’s upper and lower edges.
